Saltline Patrol is an original archipelago flight game: fly four gates over islands, drop supplies on Gull Isle, then land on runway 27 with gear down at the right speed and sink rate. Throttle, stall, fuel and damage all matter. Chase and cockpit views, live instruments, and synthesized engine and wind audio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Press Enter to start, W and S for throttle, A and D or arrow keys to bank and pitch, C to switch views, G for gear, P for the supply drop, M to mute, and R to restart. Add `?shot=cover`, `?shot=approach`, `?shot=cockpit` or `?shot=bank` to jump to a real moment of play for the screenshots.
